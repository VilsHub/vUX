vUX · Marquee

A strip that never ends.

Give Marquee a container of items. It clones them until the strip is covered, measures one loop to the pixel and scrolls it forever, with no seam and no duplicated markup in your HTML.

const m = new Marquee(box); m.config.speed = 60; m.initialize();

Hover the strip, or tab into it, and it holds still. Keyboard focus counts as hover, so a visitor tabbing through links inside a strip can read them.

    






    

        
02Any markup, links included

        
Items can be anything. The clones are marked aria-hidden, their links get tabindex="-1" and they lose their ids, so a screen reader reads each card once and Tab visits each link once, while every card on screen stays clickable.

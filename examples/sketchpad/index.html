  • Double-click empty space to type; double-click text to edit it.

04Stamps from SVG

        

            createStamp(svgText) turns SVG source into something the stamp tool places with each click.
            A stamp drawn only in black is mono: once placed, the fill colour re-tints it.
            A coloured one keeps its own colours.

02Keyboard

        

            Press Tab into this row. Focusing a member shows its tip at the
            member, blur hides it, and Esc dismisses it. A focus-opened tip
            follows its member when the page scrolls; a pointer-opened one hides instead. The shown
            member carries aria-describedby pointing at the tip.

03Edges and long text

        

            The tip is fixed to the viewport and kept 4px inside the window. Point at the far left and
            far right pins: the box is pushed in from the edge, while the arrow (the
            --vtt-arrow-left custom property) keeps pointing at the pointer.

04Lifecycle

        

            destroy() removes the listeners, the tip box and the generated stylesheet and
            gives every member its title back, so the browser's own tooltip returns. A
            destroyed instance cannot be initialized again: create a new one.

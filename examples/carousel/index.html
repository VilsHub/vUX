Slides that keep their place.

Autoplay, dots, swipe and hover-pause from one constructor and two elements you already have.

01 — autoplay

It moves on its own.

Every delay + speed milliseconds the viewport slides one frame, and wraps at the end.

01

02 — dots

Jump anywhere.

One dot per slide is generated for you. Click one and autoplay carries on from there.

02

03 — swipe

Drag it sideways.

A quarter of the frame's width is enough to commit; less springs back.

03

04 — hover

Point to pause.

The pointer over the frame holds the current slide until it leaves.

← How a swipe resolves

                

                    A drag past a quarter of the frame's width commits to the neighbouring slide;
                    anything shorter springs back. The first and last slides are end stops: swipes do not wrap,
                    autoplay does.


                    When the swipe settles, the carousel marks that slide active and lights its dot, so autoplay
                    continues from where your finger left it, not from where the timer thought it was.
                

                
While swiping is on, the container carries vTouchHandler, which gives it the grab cursor and
                    touch-action:pan-y, so on a phone a horizontal swipe is not taken over by page scrolling.
